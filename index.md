# The Stormchaser Archive
This page is an archive of past campaign threads.

## Privacy Policy for the Stormchaser Roleplaying Archive

**Effective Date:** June 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the **Stormchaser Roleplaying Archive** ("we," "our," or "the Archive") handles personal information and data contained within our historical logs of play-by-post roleplaying games, originally recorded on Discord.

By accessing or browsing this archive, you acknowledge the terms outlined below.

---

### 1. Information Contained Within the Archive

Because this archive is a compilation of historical forum pages and Discord logs, it inherently contains the following data from the time that the game was played:

* **Gameplay and Narrative Text:** Roleplay posts, creative writing, and in-character actions written by players.
* **Usernames and Handles:** The Discord usernames, nicknames, and unique user IDs associated with the original posts.
* **Profile Avatars:** Publicly available profile pictures attached to posts at the time of archiving.

> **Note on Sensitive Data:** We do not intentionally collect or store real-world personal data (such as legal names, financial information, physical addresses, or phone numbers). Any such information present in the archive is there incidentally, because it was shared publicly by users in the original forum threads or Discord channels.

---

### 2. How We Use the Data

The data in this archive is processed and displayed strictly for **historical, archival, and entertainment purposes**. Specifically, we use it to:

* Preserve the collaborative story and creative writing efforts of the community.
* Provide a searchable, readable format for former players and enthusiasts to revisit past campaigns.

We **never** sell, rent, or monetise this data, nor do we use it for targeted advertising or tracking.

---

### 3. Data Retention and Security

* **Retention:** This is a permanent historical archive. Data is retained indefinitely to maintain narrative continuity, unless a removal request is approved.
* **Security:** We implement reasonable security measures to protect the hosting environment from unauthorised access, alteration, or disruption. However, please remember that the archive's contents are intended to be publicly readable.

---

### 4. Player Rights and "The Right to be Forgotten"

We highly respect the privacy and changing comfort levels of our community members. If your past gameplay is included in this archive, then you have the right to request changes.

You can request that we:

* **Anonymise your data:** Replace your original Discord username/nickname with a generic placeholder (e.g., *Player_A* or *Anonymous_Vampire*).
* **Remove complete logs:** Request the complete deletion of your contributions (note: to preserve narrative coherence for other players, anonymization is highly preferred over total deletion, but we evaluate requests on a case-by-case basis).

#### How to make a request:

To request a redaction or anonymization, please contact the Archivist at **stormchaserroleplaying@gmail.com** with links or specific details regarding the text you wish to modify.

---

### 5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our archiving methods or hosting platform. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

---

### 6. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy and the handling of archived logs, please reach out to:
